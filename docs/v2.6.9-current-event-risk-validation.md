## Deterministic behavior

Coverage and event effect are independent. Nonblocking partial coverage remains
context-only; policy-relevant missing coverage becomes ticker-local event
review. A missing or invalid bundle fails closed as `EVENT_DATA_INCOMPLETE`.
The provider-readiness axis is never mutated.

deterministic scheduled event gate changed. The prior
`integrated-shadow-23b7c365047fc27f` cohort was preserved with its one excluded
attempt and zero valid sessions. The new semantic cohort starts at zero without
