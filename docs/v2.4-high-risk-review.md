and pre-open references now make authority order explicit. No numeric gap rule,

## 4. Schemas and tests

New schemas fail closed on independent closes, policy audit, premarket data,
baseline hashes, pre-open report/runtime/events/policy, provider incidents, and
automation delivery state. Existing v2.2 and v2.3 tests remain in the same full
suite. Remaining external risk is the unavailable approved independent-close
provider; this limits operational readiness but not merge safety.
