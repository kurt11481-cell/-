# v2.6.3 Massive-Free New-Buy Migration

## Outcome first

v2.6.3 adds research-only external discovery, deterministic unsized new-buy
drafts, exact free-Web completed-close routes, and coverage-weighted staged
promotion. It does not enable a new instrument, create a risk rule, size a
position, or authorize an order.

## Starting point

- Baseline commit: `0cd086dfe127c9b25f05fc4091cf87200aad04da`.
- `main` and `origin/main` were identical and clean.
- Pre-change suite: 363 tests, all passing.
- Rollback branch: `backup/pre-free-web-new-buy-20260813-184555`.
- Feature branch: `feature/v2.6.3-free-web-staged-new-buy`.

## Architecture changes

1. yfinance remains the primary full-universe completed-daily collector.
2. Massive credentials, MCP, OAuth, subscription, and provider acceptance are
   absent from mandatory startup gates. No Massive provider was enabled.
3. External discovery produces only a proposal with all approval booleans
   false, quantity null, and order authority false.
4. A deterministic new-buy draft is produced before independent price
   evidence. READY/NEAR_TRIGGER remain visible while price evidence is pending.
5. The exact route registry records ticker, source, page, field, price basis,
   health, fingerprint, and five-session acceptance separately.
6. Web is an acquisition method, not a source-capability provider. Search
   snippets and Yahoo-origin pages remain rejected.
7. Numerical comparison continues to use the existing tolerance and
   decision-boundary resolver. Exact accepted conditional routes preserve the
   original reported price basis and expose the normalized comparison basis.
8. Promotion now has Stage 1/5/10/20/60 and exact
   ticker/decision-class/source-route scope. No stage promotes automatically.

## Seeded routes

The active universe and supplemental product references have candidate routes
for NBIS, MVLL, FSLY, BE, TOST, SOXL, NOW, SOXX, and MRVL. Every route begins
`SOURCE_ROUTE_PENDING_REVIEW`. Generic Nasdaq pages are not labeled NOCP,
issuer IR pages require explicit dated close semantics, and ETF sponsor market
price remains distinct from NAV.

## Preserved policy

- Strategy version remains `2026-08-05-v4`.
- SOXL/MVLL/NBIS maximum-loss fractions remain 20%/60%/10%.
- SOXL timing quantities remain 24/24/24/27.
- MVLL timing quantities remain 99/99/99/99.
- Confirmed-fill EMA rearm rules are unchanged.
- Watchlist approval remains different from new-buy approval.
- Portfolio state, broker facts, cash, fills, and order authority are not
  changed by this migration.

## Operational boundary

Five-session route acceptance may use historical completed sessions, but those
sessions never count toward operational Shadow promotion. An accepted route and
a Web MATCH change only price evidence. Instrument approval, entry rule,
maximum-loss rule, risk budget, verified cash, staged entitlement, quantity,
and final human approval remain independent gates.
