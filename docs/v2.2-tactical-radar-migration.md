# v2.2 Tactical Opportunity Radar Migration

## Objective

v2.2 adds a seven-symbol tactical opportunity monitor without increasing trade
authority. It answers two separate questions: whether an opportunity is
improving, and whether current policy/account facts make it eligible for human
review. A good opportunity never implies permission to execute.

## Active universe

- Leveraged semiconductor: MVLL, SOXL
- AI infrastructure: NBIS, BE
- User-defined AI-agent initial exploration: TOST, NOW, FSLY

RBRK, NET, MDB, DDOG, SNOW, CRWD, PANW, and PLTR remain in the historical
registry as inactive records and are removed from the active daily radar.

## Identity onboarding

TOST, NOW, and FSLY were verified from their 2025 Form 10-K cover pages filed
with the SEC. The canonical registry records legal issuer name, security type,
exchange, USD trading currency, source URL/type, verification time, and status.
TOST is a research candidate only; it has no new-buy or short-sale approval.

## Canonical registry authority

`workspace/policies/instrument_registry.json` is the single canonical research
registry. The older registry embedded in `stock-strategy-system/alert_policy.json`
is explicitly marked a non-authoritative operational cache and points to the
canonical path/hash. MVLL watchlist status was corrected to match the canonical
record. `validate_registry_consistency.py` compares shared critical fields.

On `POLICY_REGISTRY_MISMATCH`, the radar may keep research visibility from the
canonical registry, but every execution review fails closed. The existing
held-position hard-risk arithmetic remains independent and unchanged in
authority.

## Opportunity and execution states

Opportunity states:

`READY`, `NEAR_TRIGGER`, `WATCH`, `NOT_READY`, `DATA_BLOCKED`.

Execution states:

`ELIGIBLE_FOR_REVIEW`, `WATCH_ONLY`, `NOT_APPROVED`, `CASH_BLOCKED`,
`PORTFOLIO_BLOCKED`, `DATA_BLOCKED`.

The deterministic tests prove:

- `READY` plus no new-buy approval remains `READY + NOT_APPROVED`;
- `READY` plus explicit instrument approval but zero cash remains
  `READY + CASH_BLOCKED`;
- both retain `quantity=null` and `order_authority=false`.

## Bounded research and shortlist semantics

The output now separates `screen_survivors`, `deep_research_shortlist`,
`deep_research_completed`, and `deep_research_skipped`. A three-to-five target
does not force weak candidates into research. Tests cover five, three, and one
qualifying candidates and require machine-readable skip reasons.

Deep research runs only for READY/NEAR_TRIGGER, a material upgrade, a material
news/event/valuation change, or explicit user request. Near-trigger conditions
reuse approved EMA reclaim, breakout acceptance, pullback confirmation, event
clearance, valuation refresh, or evidence refresh concepts; no new numeric
threshold was introduced.

Forward testing then tightened this contract: untrusted context cannot promote
a deterministic technical state, arbitrary condition IDs fail closed, a
preferred candidate requires a validated research packet hash, and selection
uses ordered non-additive gates with lower verified leverage as a late
tie-break. Cash comes only from canonical portfolio state and remains an
execution blocker, not an opportunity-quality input.

## Runtime and deduplication

`workspace/state/tactical_radar_runtime.json` is separate from brokerage state.
It stores only session, opportunity/execution state, blockers, remaining
conditions, last deep-research session, fingerprints, and delivery metadata.
Updates use atomic replacement. The transition model is NEW_READY,
NEW_NEAR_TRIGGER, OPPORTUNITY_UPGRADE, OPPORTUNITY_DOWNGRADE, DATA_BLOCKED, or
NO_MATERIAL_CHANGE. Stable event keys plus session-specific delivery IDs prevent
unchanged READY notifications from repeating.

## Schedule and scope

The independent `tactical_candidate_radar` scope is lower priority than
held-position hard risk. It is designed for Tuesday through Saturday at 07:45
KST, but the official session gate controls every run. Duplicate or stale
catch-up attempts return NO_NEW_SESSION.

The future `weekly_market_discovery` idea is recorded as an inactive proposal.
It has no current research, policy, or trade authority.

## Current shadow result

The 2026-08-11 U.S. session was verified from the official NYSE 2026 calendar
and yfinance raw data were collected sequentially. No approved independent
same-session close source was available for the seven radar symbols. The system
therefore retained all seven dispositions as DATA_BLOCKED, selected no deep
research candidate, and returned DATA_INCOMPLETE. It did not confuse a data
failure with a valid no-signal result, substitute stale data,
infer a quantity, or create an order.

The session gate now requires the caller's independently verified latest
completed session to match both the requested market session and snapshot
expected-session field. Invalid/stale sessions cannot notify. CLI paths forbid
portfolio-state aliasing and restrict writes to output artifacts; schemas are
validated before write.

## Safety boundary

No brokerage connector, order scheduler, fill inference, cash invention,
quantity invention, leverage permission, short authority, or portfolio-state
mutation was added. The user remains the sole execution authority.
