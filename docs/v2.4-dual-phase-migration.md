## Incremental changes

v2.4 preserves v2.3 role and execution boundaries and adds two phases:

1. `POST_MARKET` builds the authoritative completed-session holding/risk,
   Tactical Radar, cash, redeployment, manifest, and immutable baseline.
2. `PRE_OPEN` validates that baseline and applies only overnight-event,
   indicative quote, liquidity, and fresh verified broker-state deltas.

The migration adds provider preflight and an independent-close adapter,
global/ticker failure scoping, one global outage incident, hard-risk provenance
audit, separate Risk/Strategy states, strict cash classification, zero-funding
semantics, held-ticker capital-use deduplication, premarket schemas, run-phase
dedup, automation delivery lifecycle state, and deterministic canonical-versus-
installed Skill deployment verification.

No numeric gap rule, hard-risk action mapping, quantity, leverage permission,
short permission, fill, order, or OS scheduler was added. The exact active
tactical universe remains MVLL, SOXL, NBIS, BE, TOST, NOW, and FSLY.

## Migration safety

`portfolio_state.json` remains read-only to every research/orchestration path.
Projected and unsettled proceeds are excluded from purchasing power. Premarket
quotes never enter completed-session OHLCV and cannot create daily technical or
hard-risk authority. Missing provider/event/baseline evidence remains visible
and fails closed.
