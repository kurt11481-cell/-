## Coverage semantics

Every ticker/category record retains required and checked routes, coverage
dates, checked/publication/effective/retrieval timestamps, analysis cutoff,
materiality, announcement state, coverage, event effect, refresh trigger,
point-in-time status, claim/evidence IDs, record hash, and
`order_authority=false`.

`NOT_YET_ANNOUNCED_AS_OF_CUTOFF` requires a complete official-route check.
Unchecked routes remain `SEARCH_INCOMPLETE`; absence is never converted into a
negative event claim.
