# v2.6.9 Current Event-Risk Bundle

## Outcome

The 2026-08-13 completed-session checkpoint now contains a current immutable
event-risk bundle for the direct tactical universe and the SOXX/MRVL
supplemental symbols. The bundle is evidence for human review; it does not
mutate strategy, provider readiness, portfolio facts, quantity, or order
authority.

## Authority and cutoff

- Analysis cutoff: `2026-08-14T01:24:35.982Z`
- Completed session: `2026-08-13`
- Retrieval mode: `LIVE_FORWARD_CAPTURE`
- Bundle status: `EVENT_REVIEW_REQUIRED`
- Data status: `EVENT_DATA_INCOMPLETE`

The artifact also pins the exact event-policy file hash and the active research
baseline index hash. The active baselines at capture time were NBIS, MVLL, and
SOXL; no baseline was synthesized for the remaining symbols.

Date-only publication evidence is stored at a conservative UTC end-of-day
boundary. Where an official calendar exposes no publication timestamp, the
first repository capture time is the earliest permissible knowledge time.
Such a record is not retroactively eligible for earlier historical sessions.

## Sources

Only original SEC, issuer IR, ETF sponsor, BLS, BEA, Federal Reserve, and OCC
routes were admitted. Search snippets were not stored as evidence. A missing
official event or negative-event assertion remains an explicit missing field.

ETF and underlying evidence is separated. SOXX events do not become SOXL
issuer events. MRVL events may be shown as supplemental MVLL thesis context but
do not become MVLL product-structure events.

## Forward-only archive

`workspace/state/event_risk_archive/<SESSION>/` stores:

- `event_risk_bundle.json`
- `event_checkpoint.json`

The checkpoint is append-only, refuses historical backfill, hashes the bundle
and source URL set, chains to the previous checkpoint, and records equal
portfolio-state hashes before and after capture. The first event-specific chain
starts with the 2026-08-13 session. Prior sessions were not synthesized.

## Remaining limitations

The current bundle does not pretend that silence on an issuer or sponsor page
proves no event. Current exchange halt/delisting sweeps, complete post-earnings
SEC financing/dilution sweeps, and several next earnings dates remain missing.
The deterministic result therefore retains `EVENT_DATA_INCOMPLETE` and requires
event/research review for the affected decision domains.
