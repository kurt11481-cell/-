The NBIS -10% threshold authority is verified by the explicit user instruction
dated 2026-08-12. What is not verified is an automatic mapping from that
threshold to full exit or partial reduction. v2.3 implicitly mapped a breach to
`EXIT_REVIEW`; v2.4 removes that unsupported authority.

Therefore, a completed-close NBIS breach remains visible, freezes new-buy
execution under the existing conservative portfolio rule, and becomes:

- Risk State: `POLICY_AUTHORITY_UNVERIFIED`
- Strategy State: `STRATEGY_REFRESH_REQUIRED`
- Execution State: `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`
- Quantity: `null`

Once the user explicitly approves `action_on_breach=exit_review` or
`reduction_review`, the versioned policy may map a validated completed-close
breach accordingly. Premarket quotes can never perform this mapping.
